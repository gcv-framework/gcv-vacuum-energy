## Source ontology

At the landing,

```text
Lambda_eff = Lambda_eff(K_phys)
```

is the geometric branch output, and

```text
G_mn + Lambda_eff g_mn = 8 pi G T_mn^loc.
```

Moving the `Lambda_eff g_mn` term to the right-hand side does not turn it into matter. After landing, retained local matter responds normally to curvature and local stress. Curvature-dependent EFT operators, the renormalized Einstein-Hilbert coefficient, and higher-curvature or nonlocal gravitational responses remain physical.
