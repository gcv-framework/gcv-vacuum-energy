## Current role

Conditional local quantum implementation of a supplied source rule in the minimal three-generation Standard Model with perturbative gravity, at each separately fixed finite loop and EFT order. One complete-theory matter reference and independent geometric data are inputs. The local curvature-independent matter potential evaluated at that reference, together with its complete covariant volume family, is excluded from Einstein sourcing; phase differences, state and curvature response, independent gravitational coefficients and genuinely mixed contributions remain retained. At matched renormalized inputs, finite normalization is equivalent to ordinary EFT.

The source/input rule, the selected reference and independent geometric data are supplied premises. The construction tests their consistent implementation; it does not derive the selector or the geometric data from ordinary QFT plus GR.

The worked evidence includes interacting scalar threshold controls, a finite graviton momentum-band selected/retained diagnostic, the stated complete two-loop scalar–graviton example with overlapping subdivergences and renormalization-group transport, a 45-original-Weyl parity-even gravitational response check, and a coupled Batalin–Vilkovisky source-complex contraction. The scalar–graviton example is not a complete multiloop Standard-Model calculation.

## Domain and limits

The local compatibility statement assumes a trivializable perturbative gauge/spin sector, no physical boundary, the ordinary anomaly-restoration host, a specified infrared prescription, sufficient auxiliary counterterms, and complete functional transport and matching under admitted analytic, perturbatively invertible changes of presentation. Each loop and EFT output order is fixed separately.

The result does not establish convergence, an arbitrary-bundle interacting measure, general physical-boundary or null/corner gluing, a nonperturbative completion, a microscopic selector, or the observed value/sign of the cosmological constant. Independent gravitational and genuinely mixed vacuum contributions are retained and need not be small. No additional growth, lensing, transient, baryogenesis or black-hole result follows automatically.

The corrected phase comparison retains the full phase-energy increment. An equal numerical offset in a daughter equation alone is not evidence for a distinct calibrated local response: matching the physical parent removes that local discrepancy on a common admitted history. This statement does not establish global equivalence of the compared theories.
