## Strong-field compatibility note

The fixed-sector black-hole records are side/deep compatibility audits, not new core bridge steps. The exterior no-hair record states that, on the GR-exact GCV branch, one connected membrane-free fixed-sector exterior inherits the ordinary Einstein-Λ_eff exterior and does not allow the governed exact constant vacuum mode to become smooth radial exterior hair.

The local-support baseline imports that exterior theorem and extends the black-hole audit to static spherical interiors: fixed Λ_eff contributes cosmological curvature, while local support is carried by the source-resolved Misner-Sharp mass and local-support tensor. A de Sitter-like core whose curvature differs from the fixed flux-sector value requires explicit local support, a shell, a membrane or sector transition, boundary/corner structure, or source-assignment-compatible strong-field dynamics. The exact spacetime-constant vacuum mode alone cannot be local black-hole substance or regular-core support.

The sector-birth benchmark studies a conditional downstream option: a compact finite-thickness local sector-boundary/corner transition that opens a separate child fixed sector without using the exact constant mode as local black-hole material, without smooth parent-sector `Λ(r)` hair, and without leaking child curvature or repayment into the parent exterior.

None of the black-hole records claims a new black-hole metric, a semiclassical evaporation analysis, an information-loss solution, singularity resolution, a rotating Kerr-interior calculation, full quantum-horizon microphysics, or observational detection of child-sector formation. Those questions remain outside the route map unless they force no-leak/source-assignment failure.
